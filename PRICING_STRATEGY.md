**From trader survey**: 
- 30% of traders willing to pay $8-10/month
- Mode = $10/month
- Pro tier matches survey's "freemium sweet spot"

**vs Competitors**:
- Birdeye Premium: $9.99/month (feature parity)
- Axiom: Free (but less powerful)
- Nansen: $49/month (too expensive)
- **$9.99 is perfect middle ground**

**Pricing psychology**:
- $9.99 < $10 (feels cheaper)
- Same as Netflix/Disney+ (familiar)
- Easier to expense for professionals
- Daily cost: $0.33/day (affordable)

---

### Why $19.99/month for Premium (not $14.99 or $24.99)?

**From trader survey**:
- 20% of traders willing to pay $18-20/month
- Professional tier justifies 2x price

**Value justification**:
- Real-time alerts (worth $3-5/month)
- Backtest tool (worth $2-3/month)
- Premium support (worth $1-2/month)
- 2x price = ~3-5x value

**vs Competitors**:
- Birdeye Premium Plus: $19.99 (feature parity)
- Axiom Pro: Estimated $100+ (we're cheaper)
- Nansen: $69+ (we're cheaper)

---

### Why $500+/month for Institutional API (not $99 or $1,000)?

**From trader survey**:

- Custom API access (worth $200+/month)
