## TAM Calculation Framework

### Market Sizing Formula

```
TAM = (Total Addressable Users) × (% Who Use Smart Money Tools) × (Avg Annual Revenue Per User)

Where:
- Total Addressable Users = Solana ecosystem active traders
- % Who Use Smart Money Tools = Segment adoption rates
- Avg Annual Revenue Per User = Pricing × Retention rate
```
