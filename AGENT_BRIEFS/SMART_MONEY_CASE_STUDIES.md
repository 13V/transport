## Key Insights

### What Smart Money Does Right

1. **Entry Timing**: Identifies accumulation phases, enters before viral moment
2. **Exit Discipline**: Takes profits into strength, doesn't get greedy
3. **Diversification**: 10+ holdings reduces luck factor
4. **Community Intel**: First-mover advantage from community participation
5. **Risk Sizing**: 2-3% per position means 1 bad trade doesn't end portfolio
6. **Conviction**: Holds through volatility (12-36 months for winners)
7. **Speed**: 2-6 hour decision making vs. weeks for retail

### What Dumb Money Does Wrong

1. **Entry Timing**: Buys after obvious trend (top quartile of move)
2. **Exit Discipline**: Panic sells after losses (-30% exits)
3. **Concentration**: All-in on single speculative position
4. **FOMO Driving**: Influenced by Twitter hype, not research
5. **Over-leveraging**: 10x leverage on volatile assets
6. **No Exit Plan**: Holds until zero or complete loss
7. **Speed Disadvantage**: Acts days/weeks after smart money
