## TASKS (Complete all 3)

### Task 1: Trader Surveys (12 hours)
**Survey 20 Solana traders about smart money detection**

1. Find traders via:
   - Solana Discord communities (Magic Eden, Orca, Raydium)
   - Twitter/X Solana trading community
   - Pump.fun communities
   - Active trader addresses with public profiles

2. Survey questions (15-20 min per trader):
   - "Do you follow other traders' wallets?"
   - "How do you identify who to follow?"
   - "Would you use a tool that shows you smart money wallets?"
   - "How much would you pay per month?"
   - "What features matter most?"
   - "What problems do you have now?"

3. Collect data:
   - % who would use the tool
   - % who would pay for premium features
   - Price willingness (free, $5, $10, $20, $50/month)
   - Feature priorities
   - Current tool usage

**Deliverable:** `TRADER_SURVEY_RESULTS.md` with 20 responses, analysis, and quotes

### Task 2: Total Addressable Market (TAM) Calculation (10 hours)
**Calculate how big the market opportunity is**

1. User segments:
   - **Segment A:** Day traders (scalpers) - Solana focused
   - **Segment B:** Swing traders - Solana + multi-chain
   - **Segment C:** Retail investors - Crypto curious
   - **Segment D:** Institutional/API users - Funds

2. For each segment, estimate:
   - Number of people globally
   - % on Solana
   - % who would use smart money tracker
   - % who would pay

3. Market research approach:
   - Solana Foundation community stats
   - DEX trading volume trends
   - Competitor user counts (Birdeye, Magic Eden, etc.)
   - Crypto demographic reports

4. TAM formula:
   ```
   TAM = (Total Solana traders) × (% who track wallets) × (Avg price per user)
   
   Example:
   - 2M Solana traders
   - 15% actively track smart money (~300k)
   - Avg $10/month willingness
   - TAM = $36M annually
   ```

**Deliverable:** `MARKET_ANALYSIS_TAM.md` with TAM calculation, breakdown by segment

---

### Task 3: Competitive Pricing & Revenue Model (8 hours)
**Research competitor pricing and validate our pricing strategy**

1. Competitor analysis:
   - Birdeye (features, pricing)
   - Magic Eden (features, pricing)
   - Phantom (built-in wallet features)
   - OnChain (competitor tracker)
   - Solscan (free tier model)

2. Pricing models to evaluate:
   - **Freemium:** Free basic, $5-10/mo for premium
   - **Subscription:** $5/mo, $10/mo, $20/mo tiers
   - **API:** $0.01-0.10 per wallet analysis
   - **Ad-supported:** Free with ads
   - **Hybrid:** Free + paid + API

3. For each model:
   - Projected monthly revenue (based on TAM)
   - Implementation complexity
   - User acquisition cost
   - Churn risk
   - Competitive positioning

4. Recommendation:
   - Choose 1 pricing model
   - Justify based on data
   - Revenue projections for Year 1

**Deliverable:** `PRICING_STRATEGY.md` with competitor analysis, recommended model, revenue projections
